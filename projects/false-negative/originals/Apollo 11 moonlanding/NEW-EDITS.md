The edits retain the source views' off-center subjects, equipment, shadows, scan texture and camera crosses, with framing adapted to the requested aspect ratios. AI generation can introduce local variations beyond the flag; these files are artworks rather than archival reproductions. Small American insignia on suits and spacecraft remain part of the original scenes.

All four outputs were visually reviewed against their source frames and checked for PNG integrity and exact aspect ratio. The existing artwork and source files were left untouched. Adjacent prompt files record the initial and final prompts where a correction was needed.
