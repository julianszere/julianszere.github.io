The existing Flag Over the Ruined City.png, Red Flag Over Ruined City.png, and War Flag Over Ruined City.png were inspected and not regenerated. The familiar horizontal view, lowered-flag view and smoky comparison print were excluded to avoid repeating those compositions.

Two additional distinct frames could not be completed: alternatives/1c68093308.jpg (handoff) and alternatives/Khaldei-flag-upright-vertical.jpg (flag to the right). The image tool's output safety filter rejected both twice. The handoff's second rejection named violence; the right-facing frame's rejections used the category other. No finished outputs were returned for those frames.

The original reference files and local deletions were preserved. Output dimensions and image integrity were checked, and each completed edit was visually compared with its source.

## Tilted frame refinement

[Tilted Close Frame v2](American%20Flag%20-%20Tilted%20Close%20Frame-v2.png) was selected after six prompt variations to improve the flag pattern and its orientation to the pole. It uses the original alternate exposure as its base and a [flat 48-star flag reference](references/US-48-star-flag-design.png). The flag's short hoist edge follows the pole and its stripe lanes extend away from it into the raised cloth. The tilted composition, monochrome texture and 1024 x 1536 (2:3) format remain. The first version is retained for comparison.
