# Definitive images for the film roll

25 PNG copies from the four series folders, prepared 2 November 2026. Each filename uses the source folder name and a frame number beginning at 1 within that series. All image canvases are horizontal and exactly 3:2. Original files remain untouched.

The selection contains every PNG directly inside the four series folders at export time, including the crouched-bearer image moved into the Reichstag folder. Historical alternatives, references, nested correction/refinement trials and the standalone Pyramid Construction at Giza image are excluded.

Seven portrait frames are rotated 90 degrees clockwise, placing their subjects sideways on the horizontal film canvas while retaining their entire compositions. Turn those frames counterclockwise to view their subjects upright. Existing 3:2 landscapes are copied byte-for-byte. No images are stretched or resized.

Two Lunch frames need crops: Hats Off changes from 1422 x 1106 to 1422 x 948, removing 79 pixels from the top and bottom; High-Altitude Lunch Break changes from 1426 x 951 to 1425 x 950, removing one pixel from the right and bottom.

The numbering is an editorial sequence, not a claim of historical exposure chronology:

- Apollo 11 moonlanding (7): crew portrait, module work, lunar portrait, alternate flag pose, wide flag pose, open ground, long shadows.
- Flag over the Reichstag (6): lowered flag, crouched bearer, two wide city views, tilted close view, high pole. The sequence builds toward the raised flag.
- Lunch atop a skyscraper (7): silhouettes and two workers, receding group, two lunch views, hats off, reclining workers.
- Tank man (5): distant view, crossing view, globe-light view, Franklin wide view, tight standoff. The sequence moves from the surrounding street into the encounter.

[sequence.csv](sequence.csv) maps every frame to its source and records transformations, dimensions and SHA-256 checksums. Numbering defines the order within each series; it does not prescribe an order between the four series.
