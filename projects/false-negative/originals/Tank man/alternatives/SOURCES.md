# Tank man - alternative photographs

Retrieved 2 November 2026.

Beijing, 5 June 1989. The artwork resembles Jeff Widener's viewpoint, including the foreground lamps. I did not locate his two rejected exposures. [His account](https://www.voanews.com/amp/qa-with-jeff-widener-tank-man-photographer/1930878.html) describes taking three frames. These downloads are other photographers' photographs of the same encounter, not Widener outtakes. [Franklin's interview and mounted-slide selection](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/photography/article/qa-stuart-franklins-view-on-tiananmen-square).
