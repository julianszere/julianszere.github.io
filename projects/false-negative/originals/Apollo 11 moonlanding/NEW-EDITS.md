# False Negatives - additional Apollo 11 edits

Created and revised 2 November 2026. The initial edits used the built-in image_gen tool; the final red color match uses local Python/Pillow/NumPy processing without regeneration. Six fictional artworks substitute Soviet flags throughout each frame, including flags on suits, backpacks and spacecraft wherever visible. Three landscapes are 1536 x 1024 (3:2); three portraits are 1024 x 1536 (2:3).

The four planted-flag views were corrected again to remove residual American stripes and star patterns. The final edits use the plain red cloth in Soviet Flag on the Moon.png as their design and material reference, replacing the old surface with continuous red fabric, irregular folds, and a single gold hammer-and-sickle with an outlined star near the hoist. Cloth folds were allowed to change to eliminate the old pattern. Soviet designs also replace the alternate-pose astronaut's shoulder flag and both the backpack patch and spacecraft decal in the lunar-module view. The lunar-surface portrait replaces its visible sleeve flag; the crew portrait replaces all three sleeve flags. Patches retain cloth texture and spacecraft markings remain flat decals. NASA badges and name labels are retained.

The final color pass matches the muted red in Soviet Flag on the Moon.png. It adjusts saturation, brightness and a small hue offset only within red pixels in eleven flag areas across all six images, including sleeve badges, the backpack badge and spacecraft decal. One adjustment per photograph preserves relative differences in lighting between its flags. The gold emblems and neutral fabric are excluded from the red selection. All pixels outside the selected red areas remain byte-for-byte identical, preserving the existing textures, folds, scene and composition.

The Open Ground and Working Beside the Lunar Module images received a mask repair after the initial rectangular color-selection bounds also caught warm-toned ground beside the flags. Their planted flags now use [cloth outlines](color-match/flag-outlines.json) with a softly blended boundary and a narrow edge band restricted to strongly red cloth. This restores the affected background from the pre-grade image and avoids leaving a bright ungraded hem. The color parameters and the other four artwork outputs are unchanged by this repair.

The [color-matching script](color-match/match_flag_red.py) reads a fixed pre-grade Git revision so rerunning it does not compound the adjustment. It requires NumPy and Pillow. The [adjustment report](color-match/adjustments.json) records reference and output hashes, color parameters, flag-area bounds, background-restoration counts and validation results. All six PNGs passed integrity, pixel-preservation and aspect-ratio checks and were visually reviewed against the reference. The source JPEGs and original Soviet Flag on the Moon.png remain untouched. Adjacent prompt files record the earlier generation prompts and identify the later local color pass.
