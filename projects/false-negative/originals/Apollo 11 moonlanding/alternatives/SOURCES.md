# Apollo 11 moonlanding - alternative photographs

Retrieved 2 November 2026.

Apollo 11 lunar EVA, 20 July 1969. These are separate exposures from Hasselblad 70-mm magazine 40/S. AS11-40-5874 is the closest alternate flag pose; the other five frames provide context from the same EVA, including images made by Aldrin rather than Armstrong. Publication or rejection status is not inferred. [Frame index](https://apollojournals.org/afj/ap11fj/photos/40-s.html) and [NASA captions](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/alsj/a11/images11.html).
