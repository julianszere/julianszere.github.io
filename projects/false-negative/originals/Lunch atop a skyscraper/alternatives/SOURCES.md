# Lunch atop a skyscraper - alternative photographs

Retrieved 2 November 2026.

Rockefeller Center, September 1932. Hats Off and the napping picture are alternate poses; the other two images show the photographers at work. Authorship of the famous image remains disputed, so these files are not assigned to one photographer. These are publicly reproduced photographs, not claimed to be previously unpublished negatives. [Same-session account and Rockefeller Group credits](https://www.digitalcameraworld.com/photography/books/this-famous-photo-of-new-york-workers-eating-lunch-on-a-steel-girder-has-been-seen-by-billions-but-the-daredevil-story-of-how-it-was-photographed-can-only-now-be-told).
