These are publicly available alternate frames and related photographs. Their availability does not prove that an editor rejected them or that they were never published. This is a selection of accessible scans, not a recovered complete roll for each shoot. Widener's two rejected frames were not located.

The source notes identify adjacent exposures, other moments from the same session, other photographers' viewpoints, and comparison prints. Apollo used 70-mm Hasselblad film; the four shoots should not all be described as 35-mm sequences.

Every file was checked for image integrity and visually inspected. Downloads retain their original pixels, borders, watermarks and historical retouching. [The manifest](download-manifest.json) records source pages, download URLs, dimensions, credits and checksums.
